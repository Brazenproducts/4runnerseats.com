Frequently asked questions

Are 4th gen (2003–2009) seat covers the same as 3rd gen?

The 4th gen 4Runner ran 2003–2009 on the N210 platform. Some references call this the 4th gen rather than 3rd. When shopping, look for N210 or 2003–2009 in the fitment notes.

Does Wet Okole make covers for the 2003–2009 4Runner?

Yes. Wet Okole has custom neoprene patterns for the N210 platform 4Runner. Check wetokole.com and filter by year.

Is the aftermarket smaller for the 3rd gen than the 5th gen?

Yes. The 5th gen (2010–2023) has the most aftermarket options by far. The 3rd gen selection is more limited — verify fitment carefully on any Amazon listing.

Can I use universal seat covers on a 3rd gen 4Runner?

Universal covers will technically fit but won't look clean. Custom-fit options from Wet Okole or verified Amazon listings will give a much better result.
