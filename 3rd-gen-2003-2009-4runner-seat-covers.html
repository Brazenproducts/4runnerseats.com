The 4th gen 4Runner ran 2003–2009 on the N210 platform. Some references call this the 4th gen rather than 3rd. When shopping, look for N210 or 2003–2009 in the fitment notes.

Does Wet Okole make covers for the 2003–2009 4Runner?
