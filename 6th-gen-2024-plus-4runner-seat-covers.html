Frequently asked questions

Will 5th gen 4Runner seat covers fit the 6th gen?

No. The 6th gen uses a new platform with different seat geometry. Only buy covers explicitly listed for 2024+ 4Runner.

Does the 6th gen 4Runner have seat-mounted side airbags?

Yes, depending on trim. Trailhunter and Platinum trims have seat-mounted side airbags. Verify airbag compatibility before ordering any seat cover for those trims.

How do I tell if a listing fits the 2024+ 4Runner?

Look for "2024" or "6th generation" explicitly in the listing title or fitment notes. If it only says 2010–2023 or 5th gen, it does not fit.

Is the Trailhunter seat different from the SR5?

The Trailhunter has unique interior trim. Verify fitment notes specifically call out the Trailhunter if you have that model.
