Frequently asked questions

Are 2010–2013 and 2014–2023 5th gen seat covers the same?

Not always. Wet Okole and some other brands distinguish between pre- and post-facelift 5th gen years. Check the listing before ordering.

Do 5th gen 4Runner seats have side airbags?

Seat-mounted side airbags depend on trim level. SR5 and base trims typically do not. TRD Pro and Limited trims may. Check your owner's manual.

Does the TRD Pro have different seats than the SR5?

The TRD Pro has standard 5th gen seat geometry — covers that fit the SR5 fit the TRD Pro. The seat shape is the same across trims.

Can I use Tacoma seat covers on a 5th gen 4Runner?

No. Different platforms, different seat shapes. Always verify the listing says 4Runner.
