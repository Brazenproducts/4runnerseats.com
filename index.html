Frequently asked questions

Does Wet Okole make 4Runner seat covers?

Yes. Wet Okole makes custom neoprene covers specifically for the 4Runner, sold direct at wetokole.com. They are the top premium neoprene option — no Amazon middleman.

What years does the 5th gen 4Runner cover?

The 5th generation 4Runner ran from 2010 to 2023. It has by far the most aftermarket seat cover options of any 4Runner generation.

Are 4Runner and Tacoma seat covers interchangeable?

No. Different seat shapes, different rear configurations. Always check that the product specifically lists the 4Runner, not just 'Toyota truck.'

What material is best for a 4Runner that goes off-road?

Neoprene is the best trail material — it handles mud, water, and sweat well and dries fast. Wet Okole neoprene (direct) is the premium pick. coverdream offers a waterproof option on Amazon at a lower price.

Does the 4Runner have side airbags in the seats?

The 5th gen 4Runner does have side-curtain airbags but seat-mounted side airbags depend on trim level. Check your specific trim before purchasing.
